How this site was designed

This page is for recruiters skimming between tabs and for hiring managers checking whether the work holds up. It aims to be clear in the first 30 seconds and to reward anyone who keeps reading. The calm is deliberate: restraint is a design decision too.

Principles → decisions

The look is wabi-sabi: imperfection, impermanence, restraint. Each principle had to become a concrete choice, or it was dropped.

Lighthouse 12.8, mobile profile with simulated throttling, measured 15 October 2026 before deployment. The accessibility audit runs on axe-core.

  • Every text colour checked against WCAG AA in both themes. Moss was darkened from #6B7152 to #5F6548 to pass.
  • Tested with the keyboard alone: skip link, visible focus ring, real buttons with aria-expanded and aria-pressed.
  • prefers-reduced-motion turns all motion off. Content never depends on an animation finishing.
  • No horizontal scrolling at 320px; touch targets of at least 44px.
  • Fonts are self-hosted Latin subsets (48 KB) instead of a 455 KB third-party stylesheet. That one change took Performance from 58 to 99.

Decisions and trade-offs

No carousel
Carousels hide work behind interaction, and few people get past the first slide. Everything is visible at once.
No skill bars
"Java 80%" says nothing. Plain lists are honest, and every item is something I can discuss in an interview.
Dark mode follows your system
The system setting is the best guess at your preference. The toggle is there if you want something else here, and it remembers.
Content is data
Text, projects and books live in one JSON file, so updating the site never means editing markup. A <noscript> fallback keeps the essentials.
